Noord uses the Microsoft Authenticator app for multi factor sign in. Text message codes are no longer allowed.

1. Install Microsoft Authenticator on your phone.
2. On your PC, open your Microsoft account security page and choose **Add sign in method**, then **Authenticator app**.
3. Scan the QR code with the app.
4. Approve the test notification.

**New phone?** Ask IT to clear your old sign in methods first, then set up the app again. IT asks for approval before clearing them.
