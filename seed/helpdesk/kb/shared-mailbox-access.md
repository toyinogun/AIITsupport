Teams such as Sales and Customer Service share mailboxes (for example `sales@noord.example`).

1. Your manager asks IT to add you to the mailbox.
2. After IT confirms, close Outlook and open it again. The mailbox appears under your own within an hour.
3. In Outlook on the web, right click **Folders** and choose **Add shared folder**.

Replies sent from a shared mailbox show the mailbox name, not yours.
