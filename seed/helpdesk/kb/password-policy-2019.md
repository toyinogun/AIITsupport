Your Noord password must follow these rules:

- At least **8 characters**.
- At least one capital letter, one number, and one symbol.
- You must change it **every 90 days**. Windows reminds you 14 days before it expires.
- You cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords.

If your password has expired, press Ctrl+Alt+Del on your PC and choose **Change a password**.
