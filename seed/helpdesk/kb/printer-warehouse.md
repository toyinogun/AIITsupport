The label printers at the loading docks print shipping labels from the planning system.

- **Blank labels**: the label roll is in upside down. Turn it over and press Feed.
- **Labels print in the wrong place**: hold Feed for 3 seconds until the printer measures the roll again.
- **Nothing prints at all**: check the printer shows Ready, then restart the scanner PC next to it.

Still broken? Raise a ticket with the dock number. Your shift lead has spare rolls.
