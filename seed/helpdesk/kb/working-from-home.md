Office staff may work from home up to two days a week, by agreement with their manager.

- Connect to the VPN for shared drives and the planning system. Email and Teams work without it.
- Lock your laptop when you step away (Windows key + L).
- Do not print Noord documents at home.
- Problems? Raise a ticket or message the IT assistant in Slack.
