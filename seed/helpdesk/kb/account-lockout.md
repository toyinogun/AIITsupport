Your account locks after **5 wrong passwords within 15 minutes**. This happens to everyone, often on Monday mornings or after a holiday.

What to do:

1. Wait **15 minutes**. The lock clears itself, then try once more, slowly.
2. Still locked, or you need to work now? Raise a ticket in the helpdesk or message the IT assistant in Slack, and IT unlocks you.
3. If you have forgotten your password, see *Reset your password*.

Tip: a phone or tablet with an old password saved in its mail app keeps trying in the background and locks you again. Update it there too.
