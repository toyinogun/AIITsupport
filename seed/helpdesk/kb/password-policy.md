From February 2025 Noord follows the current national guidance on passwords.

- Use at least **14 characters**. A short sentence of four or five words works well.
- Mix upper and lower case and add a number.
- There is **no forced change** any more. Change your password only if you think someone else knows it.
- Never use your Noord password for another website.

Your password works for Windows, email, the helpdesk, and the VPN. If you change it, use the new one everywhere.
