Forgotten your password? IT can set a temporary one for you.

1. Raise a ticket in the helpdesk (from a colleague's PC if you cannot sign in) or ask Ruben Mulder in IT.
2. IT checks it is really you, then gives you a temporary password.
3. Sign in to Windows with the temporary password. You are asked to choose a new one straight away.
4. Sign in to email on your phone again with the new password.

The temporary password works for 24 hours only.
