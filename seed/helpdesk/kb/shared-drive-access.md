Each department has a folder on the S: drive. Access is given per group, read only or read and write.

1. Ask the folder owner to approve your access.
2. For the Finance folders, ask **Thomas Meijer** in Finance, who keeps the list of who may see what.
3. IT adds you to the group. Sign out of Windows and back in to see the folder.

Never copy Finance or HR files to your own drive or a USB stick.
