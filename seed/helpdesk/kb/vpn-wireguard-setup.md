Since October 2024 Noord uses WireGuard for remote access. The old VPN client no longer works.

1. Install WireGuard from the software portal (Windows) or the app store (phone).
2. Ask IT for your personal tunnel file. You only need it once per device.
3. In WireGuard choose **Import tunnel from file**, select the file, and press **Activate**.
4. Shared drives and the planning system now work as in the office.

Do not share your tunnel file. It belongs to one device.
