To work from home you need the SecureLink VPN client.

1. Download SecureLink from the software portal.
2. Install it and restart your laptop.
3. Open SecureLink and enter the server address `vpn.noord.example`.
4. Sign in with your Noord username and password.

The SecureLink icon in the taskbar turns green when you are connected.
