If you have forgotten your Noord password:

1. Send a ticket to IT, or ask a colleague to raise one for you.
2. IT confirms who you are and sends you a reset link or a temporary password.
3. Choose a new password of at least 14 characters (see *Password policy*).
4. Update the password on your phone and tablet, or they will lock you out.

A reset link is valid for one hour.
