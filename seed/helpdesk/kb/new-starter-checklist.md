Managers, please send IT these details at least **5 working days** before a new colleague starts:

1. Name, job title, department, and start date.
2. Which shared drive folders and mailboxes they need.
3. Whether they need a laptop or use a shared PC.

For new starters in Sales, also ask **Mark Dekker**, who sets up their CRM account and phone.

IT creates the account and gives the manager a temporary password on the first day.
